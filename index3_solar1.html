About Me

I have always believed that the most beautiful things we own are our memories — the small details that complete the story. Yet over time, human memory can fade or lose accuracy. That's where data comes in.

My passion for Data Engineering was born from this idea. To me, data is not just numbers or tables; it is the institutional and technical memory that preserves truth, documents decisions, and captures experience.

I strive to build robust data systems that keep information organized, secure, and accessible at any time.
